# Open questions and the assumptions I went with

In a real team these would go to the PM before building. Here I've written down the question,
the assumption I committed to, and why, so a reviewer can disagree with a specific call.

| #   | Question for PM                                                                                                                                              | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Why / impact if wrong                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | "Paris": France or Texas? How do we handle ambiguous names?                                                                                                  | Use Open-Meteo geocoding and take the top result (it's population-ranked). Accept an optional ISO `countryCode` to disambiguate, and **return the resolved location** so the caller can see what we picked. | A silent wrong guess is the worst outcome; echoing the resolved place makes it visible. A "did you mean" list is a cheap follow-up.                                                              |
| 2   | What does "rank" mean: rank days, or rank activities?                                                                                                        | Both. Score **each day 0–100 per activity**, then rank the **activities** for the week. Each activity also reports its best day.                                                                            | Answers both "what should I do this week" and "which day".                                                                                                                                       |
| 3   | How do we roll 7 daily scores into one weekly score?                                                                                                         | **Average of the top 3 days.**                                                                                                                                                                              | A plain mean punishes a week with three perfect ski days and four bad ones, when you only need a few good days. Easy to change (one function).                                                   |
| 4   | Skiing in a city with no ski slopes (e.g. London)?                                                                                                           | We score **weather suitability only**, because weather data can't tell us whether a resort exists. Elevation from geocoding is used as a soft signal. Documented as a known limitation.                     | A resorts dataset would fix this; it's out of scope for the exercise.                                                                                                                            |
| 5   | Surfing inland (e.g. Madrid)?                                                                                                                                | Use the **Open-Meteo Marine API** (wave height, period, swell). If it has no data for the point, surfing is **NOT_APPLICABLE**, not 0.                                                                      | "Not applicable" and "terrible" are different answers for a user.                                                                                                                                |
| 6   | What makes indoor sightseeing "good"?                                                                                                                        | A high baseline every day (museums are open in any weather), boosted when outdoor conditions are poor.                                                                                                      | It's the fallback activity, so it should rank top on a washout day.                                                                                                                              |
| 7   | Whose "day"?                                                                                                                                                 | The **city's local timezone** (`timezone=auto`), not UTC.                                                                                                                                                   | Otherwise "Monday" in Sydney is half of Sunday.                                                                                                                                                  |
| 8   | Units?                                                                                                                                                       | Metric (°C, km/h, mm, m).                                                                                                                                                                                   | It's the Open-Meteo default, and unit conversion is a presentation concern.                                                                                                                      |
| 9   | How fresh must the data be?                                                                                                                                  | Serve cached data up to **3h** old, or up to **24h** old if the provider is down (flagged `isStale`).                                                                                                       | Open-Meteo models update every few hours, so fetching more often adds load without new information.                                                                                              |
| 10  | Should users see _why_ a day scored low?                                                                                                                     | Yes. Each day returns human-readable **reasons** ("gusts 70 km/h, -25").                                                                                                                                    | Explainable scores build trust and make tuning easier.                                                                                                                                           |
| 11  | Multi-tenancy / auth?                                                                                                                                        | **Not in scope.** A single public API; rate limiting is left to the gateway.                                                                                                                                | Weather isn't tenant data. The README notes what tenancy would add.                                                                                                                              |
| 12  | Open-Meteo usage terms?                                                                                                                                      | The free tier is non-commercial with a daily limit, so we cache aggressively (and any load testing must use a mock upstream, never the real API).                                                           | Respecting the provider is part of a production mindset.                                                                                                                                         |
| 13  | Indoor sightseeing scores EXCELLENT even in a sunny week, because its weekly score uses its best 3 days, which are the wettest. Is that the message we want? | Kept as-is for now: "indoor is great on the rainy days" is true and actionable.                                                                                                                             | If the PM prefers "recommend indoor only when outdoor is poor", we could rank indoor by its _average_ or cap it below outdoor when outdoor is GOOD+. That's a one-line change in config/ranking. |
